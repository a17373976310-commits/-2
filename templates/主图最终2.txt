1.  **Chinese First (中文优先)**:

    -   指令：`Render [icon_style_2] with metallic sheen, and [icon_connect_2] as a precise laser line.`

3.  **Light & Material Purity (光影材质纯净度)**:
    -   指令：`Hero product with independent 6500K studio lighting, ensuring its true color and metallic sheen (like chrome or anodized aluminum) are perfectly preserved. NO red ambient light contamination on the product surface.`

4.  **Dynamic Layout (版式灵动)**:
    -   指令：`Text and icons are high-end UI elements, floating above the 3D scene. They cast no shadows on the product.`
